# KirinDL v3.2.0.12

Latest · 14 October 2026

## Custom downloads: keep video and audio together

- Custom video selections now include a separate audio track automatically when needed. The default audio follows the track selected by yt-dlp for Preset when that track is available.
- Selecting another audio track keeps the chosen video. Explicit audio choices are preserved.
- Add an Include audio control. A silent video requires switching this off; a missing audio track is shown before starting instead of silently accepting a video without sound.
- The selection summary and Download & Merge button now reflect the actual tracks, including audio paired automatically.
- Stop forcing Custom merges into MP4. Honor the user's MKV setting; otherwise let yt-dlp choose a container for the selected codecs, as it does for Preset.

## Validation

Regression tests cover Bilibili video/audio pairing, matching Preset's audio, explicit track selection, audio-only jobs, intentional silent video, missing audio and preserving the MKV setting. Existing download, Bilibili recovery, playback and YouTube tests also gate the signed APK build.

This fixes paths where Custom could omit audio or use a different container policy from Preset. Please retry an affected Custom selection to confirm sound throughout the finished video; already downloaded silent files are not changed automatically.
